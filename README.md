## About Me

👩🏻‍💻 I'm a CS PhD candidate at UIUC's [Conversational AI Lab](https://uiuc-conversational-ai-lab.github.io/), advised by [Prof. Dilek Hakkani-Tür](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=GMcL_9kAAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate).

🔬 My research focuses on AI Evaluation and Trustworthiness, especially in dialogue and user-centered settings. My work draws on both NLP and HCI to find what can unexpectedly happen after AI systems are deployed and used by real, non-malicious users, why it happens, how we can operationalize it, and how we can fix it.
